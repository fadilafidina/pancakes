ha. got you. this is a crepe recipe.

  • 2 eggs
  • 150 ml milk
  • 150 ml water
  • 125 g flour
  • a splash of vanilla
  • a pinch of salt
  • a little sugar
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted

whisk it all together and let it rest. best if it sits in the fridge overnight. butter a hot pan, pour thin, swirl. flip when the edges lift. finish with freshly squeezed lemon juice and powdered sugar.
